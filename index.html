Retracing Intelligence

Building brain evolution, one breakthrough at a time.

An interactive companion to A Brief History of Intelligence by Max Bennett. Each layer re-implements one of the five evolutionary breakthroughs as a runnable agent, sharing the same 2D foraging world so they can be compared directly.

Layer 01

Steering

Bilaterians · ~550 million years ago

A reflex agent with klinokinesis, a six-scalar affective state, and primitive associative learning. No prediction, no world model — and yet the full cycle of drive, pursuit, satiation, and even Pavlov-style conditioning falls out.
